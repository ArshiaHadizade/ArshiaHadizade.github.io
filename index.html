About

Arshia Hadizade is a psychology researcher interested in psychoanalysis, criminal psychology, criminology, and the psychological analysis of criminal behavior.

His research interests include psychological processes, unconscious dynamics, personality, criminal behavior, and the development of theoretical frameworks for criminal case analysis.

Psychoanalytic Criminal System (PCS)

The Psychoanalytic Criminal System (PCS) is a proposed integrative theoretical framework for criminal case formulation and dynamic analysis.

PCS integrates psychoanalytic perspectives with psychological formulation and criminal profiling in an attempt to provide a structured framework for analyzing psychological dynamics underlying criminal behavior.

The framework is presented as a theoretical model intended to generate empirically testable hypotheses, rather than as an empirically validated system.
